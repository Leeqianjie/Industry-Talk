## 🎤 Industry Talk – From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business

### Overview

As part of **SECP1513 – Technology and Information System**, I participated in an industry talk titled **“From Developer to CEO: The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business.”**

The webinar featured **Assoc. Prof. Dr. Hj. Mohamad Hafis Izran bin Ishak**, CEO of AR Mechatronics PLT.

- **Event:** TIS Webinar – Industry Talk 1
- **Date:** 26 October 2026
- **Time:** 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM
- **Platform:** Cisco Webex
- **Assignment:** Assignment 2 – Poster on Industry Talk

### Assignment Description

For this assignment, my group was required to design an A4 poster based on the industry talk. The poster included an executive summary, introduction, content related to the issues discussed during the talk, and personal reflection.

The objective was to understand the relationship between technology and business and how ICT professionals can contribute meaningfully to the industry.

### Key Learning Outcomes

- Gained exposure to the connection between technology and business.
- Learned about the importance of understanding both technical and business perspectives.
- Recognised the role of ICT professionals in supporting industry needs.
- Improved my ability to summarise information and communicate ideas through poster design.

### Personal Reflection

This industry talk helped me understand that working in the technology field involves more than just programming and technical knowledge. ICT professionals also need communication, leadership, problem-solving and business understanding.

I realised that technology can create greater value when it is aligned with business goals and real-world needs. As a Computer Science student specialising in Computer Networks and Security, this motivated me to continue improving my technical skills while developing a broader understanding of how technology is used in industry.

### Skills Developed

- Communication and presentation
- Information summarisation
- Poster design and visual organisation
- Critical thinking and reflection
- Understanding the relationship between technology and business

### Conclusion

Overall, this assignment provided an opportunity to explore the importance of ICT in the business world. It strengthened my understanding that successful technology professionals should combine technical expertise with communication, leadership and business awareness.
